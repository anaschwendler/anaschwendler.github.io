About me

 Posted on January 1, 1  |   2 min  |   335 words  |   Ana Schwendler  • Other languages: Português

Olar 👋 I’m Ana, a Brazilian Senior Backend Engineer at Vinted, living in Berlin.

I see my role in tech as two things: bringing more diversity to the field (to change what my university classes used to look like), and contributing to open source (to give back all the help I’ve received over the years).

At Vinted I’m part of the Ontologies team, which shapes how millions of items are organised and found on the marketplace. Most of my work is taking catalog domains out of a large Rails monolith and turning them into standalone services in Go, built to handle marketplace-scale traffic. Before that I spent a decade in backend and data work: logistics integrations at Marley Spoon, internal tools at Tourlane, analytics at Mailbutler, and data science for Operação Serenata de Amor, a Brazilian open source project that used machine learning to flag suspicious public spending.
